Minor: the hand-off's token chip and icon tile. Chip `token` is the tag of a colour role (a 32 px pill in the card fill inside the hairline, a 500 12 px mono label, 6 px beside its 20 px swatch), and Chip `square` holds an `option` chip to the 30 px tile an icon-only option takes, its label the tile's 500 10 px mono line. Swatch `tiny` is the 20 px disc that chip carries, and Swatch `ring` draws the edge as a 1 px inset ring in the field border in place of the hairline.
