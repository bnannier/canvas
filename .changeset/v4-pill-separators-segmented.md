Divider `inset` and Navbar `brandDivider`: new public options. A `vertical inset` Divider is the hand-off's short 1 x 18 px separator centred in its row with 4 px on each side, and `brandDivider` draws one between a Navbar's brand and its links. Divider's emphasis now follows the hand-off (the `border` hairline by default, `field-border` when `strong`, the hairline at 60% when `soft`), and the web ButtonGroup segmented control takes the hand-off's look: a 10 px `secondary` track with a 3 px inset, 7 px segments, a flat `card` thumb on the sm shadow, 500 labels, 30 / 36 / 44 px tall, 8 px padding for an icon alone; under glass the group takes the control layer and its selection puck the segment's corners.
