Adds `DropZone`'s `bordered` prop (justification for the minor: a new public option): a bordered zone is the Canvas v4 hand-off's dashed drop well with its 11px mono label, lit in the accent wash and edge while an item hovers it; without it a zone keeps the bare overlay it had.

The Surfaces & data components take the Canvas v4 hand-off's look. Stats is the 16-corner card tile, 18 in (12 compact), its 600 28/20/36 figure in the display face tracked -0.02em over a 500 12 subtle label and a mono delta; the plain surface sets the tiles edge to edge in one card behind hairline rules. DataTable is framed by default at 14 (`attached` drops the frame) with a 500 12 muted header band on the popover fill, 400 13/18 cells and the accent sort arrow; Android takes that table with its 48dp targets and the ripple, iOS keeps its SwiftUI Table. StackedList frames at 14 under the small shade with 16 by 12 rows, a 500 14 name and a mono meta. GridList's gallery tile is a 14-corner card with a 110 thumbnail over its caption. MediaObject leads with the 40px icon box at 11 on the accent wash. Feed draws 400 13/19 lines with a 600 actor and a 12 subtle timestamp beside its node on a field-border connector: an item's glyph on a 28px secondary disc, otherwise a 10px dot (the first in the accent) where it showed the actor's initials, and the avatar lead's 28px avatar on the same connector rows where it had hairline-ruled rows; its activity and trace times are the 12 mono caption. DescriptionList is the inset card, 16 by 20, rows 12 above and below with a 500 13 muted term beside a 400 14 value under a 500 16 title, and Android draws that list; iOS keeps its grouped value rows. ActionPanel is a 16-corner panel 18 in with a destructive wash; Accordion is the 16 card 18 across; Collapsible a 14 card with a trailing chevron; CodeBlock a 14 card with a 36px mono header; Carousel has 32px arrows and accent pill dots; Video and QRCode sit in a 14 frame; Board lanes are sunken 14 wells holding 10-corner cards; Chat is a framed thread with soft 16 bubbles tailed at 4 and a sunken composer well; Calendar has 38px cells with a ringed today. Neither iOS nor Android ships a control for the stats, feed, lists, media object, panels or board, so their native skins draw the hand-off's parts in SF Pro and Roboto with each platform's press feedback.
