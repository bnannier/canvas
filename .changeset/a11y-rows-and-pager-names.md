Two new capabilities from the v4 hand-off's component page, the user-visible additions
this minor carries. DescriptionList gains `ruled` on its rows axis (it wins over
`divided`): every row, the first included, draws a 1 px top rule in the `border` token,
pads 9 above and below with nothing beside, and in the two-column layout the term hugs
its content over a 120 floor with 14 to the value, the hand-off's accessibility-card
rows, so a list under a caption reads as a table that starts below it. Typography gains
`pagerName`, the previous and next cards' name under their label: the display face, 500
18 on the face's own 22 line (the hand-off sets no ratio; the line is Chromium's `normal`
for Bricolage Grotesque, measured on the hand-off page), in the foreground ink. The docs'
accessibility cards take `ruled` and the previous/next cards take `pagerName`.
