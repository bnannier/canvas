Adds `ChipGroup`, a pick-one set of chips (justification for the minor: a new public component). Its `<Chip value="…">` options form one WAI-ARIA radiogroup: radio chips with `aria-checked`, one roving tab stop (the chosen chip, or the first enabled one), the arrow keys moving and selecting with wrap, Home and End, and disabled chips skipped, through the shared roving-focus hook RadioGroup and Tabs use. It follows RadioGroup's contract: `value` / `defaultValue` / `onChange`, `disabled`, a `label` heading with a `description`, plus `accessibilityLabel` to name a group whose heading is already on the page. `Chip` gains the `value` prop that makes it an option.

Chip, Kbd, Radio, Slider, Stepper and Avatar take the Canvas v4 hand-off's look. Chip is the 28px outlined pill at weight 500: a palette hue is its 16% oklch wash inside the same hue at 30% under an ink held to 4.5:1, `primary` the solid accent, `secondary` the higher raised fill, a selectable chip at rest the bare pill in the field border and selected the accent wash inside an accent edge, with a mono count and an 18px remove disc; Material 3's 32dp chip keeps its shape on Android. Kbd is the 24px mono keycap with a 2px bottom edge; a combo sits as caps with no "+", a sequence reads "then" between caps. Radio draws the hand-off's thick-ring mark on the web and keeps the iOS checkmark list and the Material 3 radio, with the hand-off's gaps. Slider is the 4/6/8px raised track with a glowing accent fill and a white thumb that takes an 8px accent halo under the pointer or a press. Stepper is one bordered 10px-corner box with a mono value and its description under the control. Avatar's sizes are 20/28/40/56 (`tiny` was 24 and `large` 48), `rounded` corners are 28% of the disc, initials sit at weight 600 over the identity hue's soft wash with an ink held to 4.5:1, and the stock `glow` is a page gap inside the accent glow; Argus's reference sizes keep their gradient.
