Image and CardMedia announce their `alt` text on the web: react-native-web dropped it, leaving the picture unnamed. A named Image is exposed as an image; an unnamed one stays decorative. A labelled BadgeGroup is a named group, a vertical Divider says it is vertical, and a labelled Divider is named by its label. A tappable MediaObject keeps its trailing action beside the row instead of inside its button, and the row is named by every line it shows; with both `onPress` and an `action`, `testID` marks the card that holds the two, so a test that pressed the row through it now finds the row by its button role and name. ActionPanel's toggle is the Switch's own row (the title its label, the description its muted line, the whole row the tap target), so its copy takes the Switch's type (a 14 px medium title over a 12 px description) and `destructive` no longer reddens a toggle's title; an `inline` panel stacks its action under the copy when it is narrower than the `md` measure. Breadcrumb's `maxItems` JSDoc now says it keeps the last `maxItems - 1` crumbs, as it always has. The test suite also mounts every platform-entry export in the skins smoke test, owns QRCode, DepthChart and five charts' dev warnings, and the audit facts credit RevealGroup.
