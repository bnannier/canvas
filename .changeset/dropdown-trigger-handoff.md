Dropdown-class triggers hand off to their menu under glass: the outline button, the AvatarMenu capsule and the collapsed Navbar hamburger give their glass pill to the menu's material as it blooms on the pill's frame, and the menu shrinks back to re-form the pill before the label fades in on close, the way the iOS 26 menu behind a toolbar button opens and dismisses. One mechanism on the native Liquid Glass, the web lens and the frost; the field popups (Select, Autocomplete) keep their field visible and never hand off.
