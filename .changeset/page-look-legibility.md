Legibility over Dark Factory's page look. The muted ink is solved to 4.5:1 on the frosted shell over the page gradient and its orbs as well as on every surface, so it darkens slightly in the light palettes (blush #696b8d to #606183, mint #5f7083 to #5a6b7e) and lightens slightly in dark (#a3a5c6 to #a6a8c9). A brand-tinted glass fill (a primary Button, a checked control under glass) is solved over the frame's beds as well as the page token (`frameBeds`, derived from Dark Factory with the tokens), so in dark it is a few percent denser and its ink keeps 4.5:1 there. The iOS ActionSheet's secondary header label is foreground at 0.89 instead of 0.85, which keeps it at 4.5:1 on the glass sheet.
