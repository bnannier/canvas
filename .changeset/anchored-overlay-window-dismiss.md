A tap anywhere off an open menu, list or popover now closes it, also when the card opens inside a nested `OverlayProvider`. The dismiss layer used to fill only the nearest provider, so on a docs example stage (and in any app that nests a provider around a panel) a tap beside that provider left the card open on iOS, Android and the web. A card that can close now paints, with its dismiss layer, in the outermost provider of its window, at exactly the place its nearest provider gives it, so the tap closes it and goes no further, the way a platform menu behaves; while it is open the page under it takes no touches and does not scroll. An open card also follows its trigger when the content under it moves without a touch (a keyboard or programmatic scroll, a layout shift), where it used to stay behind in an app whose provider sits outside its scrolling content; a card opened from inside another card is placed by the same provider as its parent. A card pinned open with no `onOpenChange` still paints in its nearest provider, and a provider in a separate window (a `Drawer`, or `separateWindow`) remains its own window. Portaled content now resolves the `BreakpointOverride` of the component that opened it, wherever its provider sits.
