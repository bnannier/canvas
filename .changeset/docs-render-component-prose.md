Component pages now show every line of their `.md`. The docs generator reads each page into one document model (`parseDoc`), and the page renders all of it: the intro after its first paragraph as an overview under the lead, the prose written beside an example as its note under the Playground rail while it is selected (and the prose before the first variant as a note on every example), Do and Don't captions whole with their code spans in the mono face and their strong runs at semibold, and the guidance sections after Do & Don't with their fences as live examples (Button's "Real links (href)" and "Touch area", Chip's "Touch area", Typography's "Inline links (href)"). A bullet list in that prose is a real list, `ul` of `li` on the web, as the theming page's bullets now are too. The page gate gains two rules: S7 (a section of the page's own goes after Do & Don't, holds something, holds no Do/Don't pair, and takes a name of its own) and S8 (every non-blank line reaches the page as written, so a line the model drops, or a link, emphasis, ordered list, table or deep heading the page cannot render, fails `docs:gen`), and S2 now requires the intro to open with a paragraph. The e2e routes and the audit inventory read the same examples as before, through `splitDoc`, now a projection of the model. Repository tooling and docs only; nothing in the package changes.
