The Charts section cards' pieces (justification for the minor: new public options). The chart value flag is the inverse ink pill (the Tooltip bubble's reading, mono 11 px, the `chartFlag` corner, the dense inverse tint under glass), and the cartesian charts mark the inspected category on the axis in the card ink with a dashed `3 4` guide. New options: `Chart` `formatValue`; `ChartSeries` `dashed` (LineChart, ComposedChart, CandlestickChart overlays, with a dashed legend rule); `CandlestickChart` `legendCaption`; `ProgressRing` `stacked`; `MetricBreakdown` `hideSparkTag`; `BulletChart` per-row `success` / `warning` / `destructive`; `DepthChart` `spread`; `UptimeBar` `midLabel`. Looks taken from the hand-off: the selected bar's gradient and glow, the ring and gauge arc glows, the completed ring's check, the radial bar's first series on the outer ring, the radar's quarter rings, the range area's selection marks, the scatter plot's solid selected point, the box plot's half-width whisker caps, the sparkline's range-scaled bars, the calendar heatmap filling its width, the treemap's 2:1 tiles and near-black ink, and the geo map's stacked zoom control.
