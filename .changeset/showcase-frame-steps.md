Adds the steps the Canvas v4 section pages' showcase frame is built from (justification for the minor: new public options). Card takes two corner roles beside the component page's, `showcaseCorners` (20 on the web, the hand-off's section page card) and `heroCorners` (24 on the web, its section hero's stage), the native skins keeping their own card corner on them as on the component page's steps; a `roomy` density (a 24 px inset with 20 px between flat children, the section card's, on every platform); and `heroShadow`, the medium step of the shadow ladder (the hand-off's `--sh-md`) between the resting card and the raised one. Row, Column and Grid take `ample` (40), the step between a section page's hero and its body and between the hero's halves, under `broad` (48), and Row and Column `rowsPanel` (28), the vertical gap of a wrapped or stacked row apart from its main gap.
