DataTable: full-featured build-out. Columns accept descriptors (`{ label, key, numeric, centered, width, sortable, sortValue }`) alongside plain strings, for per-column alignment, fixed widths, and sorting. Header-press sorting cycles ascending, descending, off (table-level `sortable` or per-column; `sort`/`defaultSort`/`onSortChange`; numeric-aware comparator; `aria-sort` + per-platform direction icons). `selectable` is now real row selection: interactive per-row checkboxes plus a select-all header checkbox with an indeterminate partial state, keyed by `rowKey` (`selectedKeys`/`defaultSelectedKeys`/`onSelectionChange`), and a row press toggles selection when no `onRowPress` is set. `paginated` adds a built-in footer (the kit Pagination): a "Showing X-Y of N" range, `pageSize`/`pageSizes` (rows-per-page selector), a controlled or uncontrolled `page`, and the selection count. `loading` renders pulsing Skeleton placeholder rows; `emptyMessage` renders centered under the header when there are no rows; `comfortable` completes the density axis. Selected rows tint with the platform's selection fill (M3 primary state layer on Android, system fill on iOS, accent on web). Checkbox: new `accessibilityLabel` prop for label-less boxes (used by the table's selectors).
