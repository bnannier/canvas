A loading Button no longer exposes an unnamed progress bar: react-native-web draws its spinner as a `progressbar` with no name, which assistive tech met as a second control inside the button, so the spinner is now hidden from assistive tech the way Spinner hides its renderer, and the button itself stays a named, busy button. Form takes an optional `accessibilityLabel` that names its form landmark (ARIA asks every form landmark for a name, and it is how two forms on one page are told apart); without it a Form renders exactly as before. The docs follow: the catalog's category headings are h2 under the page's h1, its two forms are named, and its ScrollView tile scrolls a bar of filter chips a keyboard can reach. The Activity and Team templates keep their search and email fields beside a Select that hugs its value, where a filling Select had squeezed the field to nothing and lay over it. The docs mark the look a page has committed (`data-look` on the root element), and the browser suite waits for it, since a link's scheme and surface now land after hydration and a dark page could be scanned while it still showed the server's glass. The structure check expects the Do & Don't section heading the component pages render.
