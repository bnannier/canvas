The Foundations section cards' pieces (justification for the minor: new public options on existing components). Swatch takes the `tile`, `bar` and `steps` forms (with `segmented`), and the `fluid`, `glow`, `centered`, `bare` and `compact` lockups, with an `ink` for a tile's name; its name and value lines sit on their faces' normal lines (16 and 14 px). Icon takes the `subtle` tone (the second ink) and the `cell` glyph tile with its web hover wash, and `muted` now paints the third ink as the hand-off's renderer does. Emblem takes the `cyan` and `secondary` tones, Card the `sample` elevation tile and `floating` (the floating glass material on the web), and Badge `dot` with no label draws the bare 8 px dot.
