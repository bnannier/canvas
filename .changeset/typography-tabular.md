Typography tabular figures for numbers read down a column or updated in place: Typography gains a `tabular` boolean, its own single-member numerals axis, that gives every digit the same width on iOS, Android and the web, so a column of figures lines up and a live metric or counter holds still as its digits change, the way Dark Factory sets its figures. It combines with every role, tone, weight, leading and decoration, and prose keeps the face's own proportional figures. With it in place, `tabularNums` is a deprecated alias (the owner's K12-2 decision) that keeps working until a major and points to `<Typography tabular>`.
