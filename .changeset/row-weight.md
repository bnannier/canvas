Row children take `weight`, a share of the Row's free width after its gaps (CSS grid's `minmax(0, Nfr)`), for the splits twelfths cannot name (justification for the minor: a new public option). `weight={1.7}` beside `weight={1}` splits a Row 1.7 to 1 whatever either side holds, the Canvas v4 component page's anatomy figure and legend. A weighted box has no content floor, replaces `fill` and `grow` on the same box, and is the whole line once a `stacks` Row has stacked; DEV warns for a weight outside a Row, a weight that is not a positive number, and a weight combined with `span`, `fill` or `grow`.
