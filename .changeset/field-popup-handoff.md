Hand the Autocomplete, Select and PhoneInput fields' glass to their lists the way the Dropdown hands its pill to its menu: under glass the list pours out of the field's edge in the field's width, corner and tone, and on close absorbs back into the field's box, the field's text yielding only while the pane covers it, so a field that is typed into never vanishes. Solid mode and Reduce Motion keep the plain tree.
