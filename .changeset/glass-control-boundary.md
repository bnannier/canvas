Under glass a selection control keeps the boundary that identifies it at 3:1, as WCAG 1.4.11 asks (the owner's GS-1 decision). An unchecked Radio ring, an empty Checkbox box and an off Switch track gave their boundary over to the glass material's rim, which measured about 1.2:1 against the page in the light palettes and 1.4:1 in dark, so an unchecked radio or checkbox and an off switch nearly vanished in light glass. Now the unchecked ring and the empty box keep their outline over the material in its solid colour (`input` on the web, `muted-foreground` on Android), with the material flush inside it, and the off Switch track keeps its off-track grey over the material, at the sheerest strength at which the track keeps 3:1 against the page and under its thumb (on iOS, Apple's systemGray3 track, as in solid mode), with its thumb in its own colour over the material too, since a frost beneath them blurred the page into the track (2.8:1 where the fill solves to 3.35) and the track into the thumb. Checked controls are unchanged: their brand-tinted glass carries them. Under Increase Contrast the kept outline takes the contrasting border like any resting border. `GlassPaneProps` gains optional `boundary` and `boundaryFill` props, `GlassSurface` an optional `boundaryFill`, and `paneStyle`'s third argument an optional `"boundary"` value for this, all on deprecated aliases; nothing else changes and solid mode is unchanged.
