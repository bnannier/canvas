Emblem gains a `warning` tone boolean on the existing tone axis (primary / destructive / success / warning / muted): the amber caution emblem from the hand-off. It follows the same recipe as the other tones, a 12 percent wash of the `warning` token behind the square and the solid token on the glyph or monogram, and slots into the fixed tone precedence after `success`.

Minor justification: new public API (the `warning` prop on Emblem), not a fix to existing behavior.
