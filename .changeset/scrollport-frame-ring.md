A keyboard-focused CodeBlock, DataTable or Carousel scroller now shows the theme's focus ring on the web instead of the browser's blue one. Each of these scrollers sits flush inside a clipping card, so it could not wear the ring itself: the card clips a ring drawn outside the scroller, and Chromium paints the scrolled content over a ring drawn inside it (a terminal's dark rows hid even the browser's ring). The card now draws the ring instead: the CodeBlock's card or terminal window, the DataTable's surface, and the Carousel's viewport with the slide's corners. It is the CSS hand-off's solid 2 px `--ring` at the kit's 2 px offset; an `attached` DataTable, which sits flush inside a parent frame that clips, draws it just inside its own edge instead. It shows only while the scroller has keyboard focus: Tab (or Safari's Option+Tab) onto it, or a key pressed on it, and it comes back when focus returns from another window. A click into the code draws no ring, just as `:focus-visible` draws none, and the scroller drops its own ring so the two never stack. iOS and Android are unchanged, since their views take no key events unless an Android app turns on React Native's `enableKeyEvents` flag.
