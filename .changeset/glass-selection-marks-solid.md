Under glass a Radio, a Checkbox and a Switch now draw their solid look on every platform, with no glass pane and no rim (the owner's GS-5 decision): they are small marks inside content, not surfaces, and neither Apple, Material 3 nor Dark Factory draws them in glass. A checked box, track or ring is its solid brand fill again (a checked Radio is its primary ring around a primary dot, where glass drew a filled brand disc with an inverse dot), an unchecked ring and box keep their solid outline and an off track its solid fill, with no grey frost inside them, and on iOS 26 the Switch knob is no longer a Liquid Glass puck. A switch row now dims while pressed and when disabled as it does in solid mode. On Android an overlay or a bridged Modal no longer mounts a capture frost for each mark, and a mark's borderless ripple there comes forward through the surface it sits in. The same holds wherever the Checkbox indicator renders: a Listbox's multiple selection, the FilterPanel's option rows and DataTable's selection column. A Radio `card` option is unchanged, a content pane with its selection wash. Solid mode is unchanged, and so is Increase Contrast. This supersedes what the pending release notes say of these three controls under glass (the full-footprint, ripple and not-interactive notes). The unreleased GS-1 additions for this case go with it: `GlassPaneProps.boundary` and `boundaryFill`, `GlassSurface`'s `boundaryFill` and `paneStyle`'s `"boundary"` value never shipped.
