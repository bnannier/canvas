On iOS's frost and Android's expo-blur frost a glass surface's tint now paints over the native blur, as it already did on the web and over Android's canvas-blur capture frost, and as Dark Factory's GlassSurface paints its fill over its BlurView. The tint used to paint beneath the BlurView, so the frost sampled the surface's own tint (against the materials law that no material samples itself) and dyed it with its light or dark veil: on the iPhone simulator a fill under the frost read up to 53 levels a channel off the same fill composited on the frost (a red diagnostic tint; up to 10 on the dark Card's content tint), where over it the fill lands within half a level of the composite. The tints, the blur intensities, the specular rim and its bands, the footprint, the clip, the corners and their curve, solid mode, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast and iOS 26's Liquid Glass are unchanged. On iOS a dark field's body now reads #373740 where it read #272730, so a field's prefix or suffix addon is capped for its ink on every platform, as the web's already was (the owner's GS-2 decision): its `$` reads 4.91:1 with no addon fill in dark, where the full-strength addon would have read 3.83.
