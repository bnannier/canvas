The Overlays section's kit pieces (justification for the minor: new public options on existing components). Alert takes `announcement`, the full-width product bar on the accent's gradient, and `action`, one inline link; Dialog takes `banded` (header, body and footer bands under rules) and `icon` (the accent's soft tile above the title); Drawer and ActionSheet take `embedded`, the open-in-place specimen that is a labelled region with no focus trap, no Escape or hardware back and a dim that passes touches through; Dropdown items take `submenu`, and a `selected` item draws the web's soft wash and leading check; Progress takes `status`, a short word in the percent's place; Emblem takes `soft`, the tone's soft wash tile; Card takes the `well` density (16 inset, 12 apart); a palette Swatch takes a size boolean for its disc (`small` is 32 px); an inline Popover given `top` or `bottom` draws its beak, at the end with `alignEnd`. The web looks follow the Canvas v4 Overlays page: AlertDialog and Toast float on the functional glass layer, AlertDialog's default width is the `sm` step and its confirm field carries its name as a placeholder, the Dialog close is the 28 px transparent box, the Wizard is 672 wide with segmented Steps in a banded header, the Command palette's `esc` hint is the compact flat cap and its list caps at 340, the alert and toast dismiss boxes round at 6, and the Spinner description sits on a 17.4 line.
