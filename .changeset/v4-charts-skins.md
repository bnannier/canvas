The charts take the v4 hand-off's look (its chart renderers in `cv-kit-charts.js`), on every platform. The chart frame pads 16 (12 compact) with a 500 14 title; axis labels are JetBrains Mono in the subtle ink, the y axis labels every other tick, and the grid is a 2 4 dotted rule with a solid zero line and no vertical lines; legends draw 8 px rounded squares in 12 px text. Bars round every corner at 3 and fill 46% (grouped) or 42% of the band; lines stroke 2.2 for the lead series and 2 for the rest, with 3 px dots and a .35 fade; areas wash at .28 and .3. Pie and donut, gauge (a radius 80 semicircle with a 0 / label / 100 scale), progress ring (168, a 14 stroke, the label beside it), radial bars, radar (field-border rings, no spokes), treemap (tiles mixed 70% over the card, with an ink that clears 4.5:1), heatmap (five chart-1 levels, 2.5 corners), candlestick, box plot, waterfall (no connectors), scatter (r 4 at .7), histogram, range area and depth chart follow the hand-off's numbers. FunnelChart draws centered rows (label, bar at its share of the first stage, mono value) in place of trapezoids. BarList draws each row as a proportional tone wash behind its label; MetricBreakdown stacks label, display headline and rate beside its trend strip over mono meter rows; StackedBar is an 8 px pill with 2 px between segments and an inline legend; UptimeBar takes a status dot header and 30 px pills; ServiceHealthList rows carry a 14-period inline strip and a status-toned mono detail, also when `compact`. No public props change.
