Minor: three new options for a documentation page. Button `jumpLink` is a page's jump to one of its sections (a 28 px hairline pill around a muted 12 px label), and Button's page links now brighten their label to the full ink on hover. Chip `option` (and ChipGroup `option`) is the compact pick of a settings panel: 26 px tall, a 12 px label, no check. Typography `pageLead` now holds a page summary to the 72-character measure.
