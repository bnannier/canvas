The docs generator now refuses a component page that is not shaped the way it reads one: `docs:gen` and `docs:gen:check` check every `src/*/*/<dir>.md` against located, numbered rules (the registry name as its title, a prose intro, `## Usage`, `## Variants` and `## Do & Don't` each once and in that order, one fence per example, every Do & Don't pair under a `###` title with a caption and its own fence, and a Don't that differs from its Do), and `bun run test` holds the pages and the docs registry to the same rules. Do & Don't now ends at the next `##`, so a section of a page's own after it is never read into the last pair. Every page passes: Image, Pressable, ScrollView, TextInput, Video, FilterPanel, AreaChart, CandlestickChart, DepthChart and ScatterPlot gain Do & Don't lessons of their own, Popover gains Inline, Description only and Controlled variants, Sparkline's two Variants sections are one, Container's "Naming a measure" Don't shows the hand-capped View it warns against instead of repeating its Do, and the untitled pairs (and Switch's second pair under "On") have titles. Repository tooling and docs only; nothing in the package changes.
