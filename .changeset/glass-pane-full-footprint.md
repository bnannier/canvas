Under glass a control's material now covers its whole solid footprint, as Dark Factory's glass does (the owner's GP-1 decision). GlassPane, the material a kit component paints behind its own content, copied its host's border widths onto itself inside a host that kept its own transparent border, so every bordered pane host drew a smaller surface under glass than in solid: a Chip, Badge, Kbd, Checkbox box, Radio ring, Steps disc, Select trigger, Alert, Card and the rest sat two border widths in (a 20 px radio ring painted a 12 px disc), and a kept focus or open border showed a see-through gap before the material's rim. The pane now takes only its host's corner radii and `borderCurve`; under glass `paneStyle` turns a resting border into padding of the same width, so nothing moves and the material reaches the edge, and a state border (a focused or errored field, an open Select, Android's current step, the neutral Alert's edge, an Avatar's separator ring) stays a border with the material flush inside it on concentric corners. Absolutely placed content keeps its solid place (the Android Switch thumb, the Android Select and Autocomplete floating labels), and every pane host on the web, iOS and Android skins keeps its solid insets, held by the new `test/glass-pane.test.tsx`. Solid mode is unchanged. GlassSurface's own hosts (the Tabs track, ButtonGroup) are aligned in GlassSurface's turn.

Two deprecated aliases gain an optional prop for this: `GlassPaneProps.stateBorder`, which sits the pane flush inside a border its host keeps (pair it with `paneStyle`'s third argument), and `FloatingLabel`'s `edges`, how far a field's padding box reaches over its border, which the label's bounds step back from. `paneStyle`'s glass result for a resting host now carries padding where the border was: an app that composes it and sets a padding after it replaces that padding, and its content sits one border width further out under glass than in solid; hand `paneStyle` the whole box instead, as the kit's components do. On the web a focused host whose border became padding draws Chromium's `outline: auto` focus ring one CSS px further out than in solid (audit GP-13, open).
