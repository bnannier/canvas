The glass state washes keep their text legible (the owner's GP-2 decision). Under glass an errored Input, Textarea or PhoneInput and a checked Radio card or selected Card painted a destructive or primary wash at 18 or 22 percent in place of the pane's own body, which dropped the muted placeholder, prefix and description on it to about 3.5:1. Each wash now lies over the pane's own body and is capped at the densest alpha at which the same inks as in solid mode, muted and foreground, keep 4.5:1 over the page and over a content pane, in blush, mint and dark: the state is carried as in solid by the error border and by the selected card's primary border, which a selected Card and a checked Radio card now keep over the material. (A later change in this release sets the selection wash to solid's own 5 percent tint in every look and leaves the error wash none.) The values live in one table, `src/style/glass-surface/glass-wash.ts`, a tuning-harness tunable.
